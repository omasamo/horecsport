# horecsport.sk: structure of the old site

These notes come from the Wayback Machine (web.archive.org). The live domain no longer resolves. The site was captured from 2006 to 2022, with the last homepage snapshot on 2022-08-12. Its newest article is dated 14.11.2018. It was a static FrontPage site built from tables, in Slovak with a little Czech.

**Who it belongs to:** *Občianske združenie Horec Sport Team* (HST), a civic association (club) of friends from Bratislava. They do mountaineering, high-mountain hiking, ski touring, ice climbing, cycling and other outdoor sports. They describe themselves as *"Partia kamarátov so záujmom o turistiku, horolezectvo, skialpinizmus, cyklistiku a ostatné športy spojené s prírodou a všeličo iné :-)"*. The site is mostly the club's trip reports and photo galleries, from the Tatras and the Alps up to the Himalayas.

## Logo and visual identity

- **Logo:** [images/brand/logo-horec-sport-team.jpg](images/brand/logo-horec-sport-team.jpg) (165×121 JPEG; only one capture exists, from 2007, so this is the highest resolution available). It is an oval badge with a black outline on white. "HOREC SPORT TEAM" runs in bold condensed capitals along the top arc and "SLOVAKIA" sits along the bottom. In the centre a blue **gentian flower** stands in front of grey mountain peaks. *Horec* is Slovak for gentian, the alpine flower the club is named after. The design needs redrawing as vector art for any modern use.

## What's in this folder

- `pages/*.md` holds the Slovak text of every archived content page as Markdown: the homepage, about pages, section indexes and about 140 trip reports. Each file starts with its source URL and Wayback snapshot. Links between pages point at the other `.md` files. Images that weren't downloaded and links to pages that weren't archived point to web.archive.org.
- `pages.json` maps each original path to its file, page title and snapshot timestamp.
- `trips.md` is the full trip log (year, title, place) parsed from the "Archív akcií" page.
- `images/brand/` holds the logo, header banner and sidebar photo.
- `images/site/` holds section thumbnails, news thumbnails, member photos and the first few photos of each trip report, mirroring the original paths.

Per-photo viewer pages (`imageNN*.htm` and numbered gallery pages) were skipped. The full gallery photos are still on web.archive.org under the same paths.
